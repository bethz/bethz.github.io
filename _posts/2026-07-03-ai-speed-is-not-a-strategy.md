AI makes everything faster. That's the pitch. That's the promise. And it's true.

But faster isn't a strategy. It's a capability.

Every company now has access to the same acceleration. Your competitors can generate the same docs, build the same prototypes, deploy the same agents. Speed is table stakes.

What separates winners from losers in the AI era isn't how fast they move. It's whether they're moving in the right direction.

I've seen teams ship AI solutions in weeks that took months to unwind. Blazing fast. Completely wrong. The speed made the mistake more expensive, not less.

Speed without direction is expensive chaos.

Direction means:
- Knowing which problem you're solving before you solve it
- Having alignment before you have velocity
- Choosing what NOT to build, even when you could build it in an afternoon
- Measuring outcomes, not outputs

The companies I work with that are winning with AI aren't the fastest. They're the most deliberate. They move at the speed of clarity, not the speed of generation.

AI gives you a faster engine. Strategy tells you where to drive.

Don't confuse the two.
