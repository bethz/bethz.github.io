Thought leadership used to mean: I have insight, I broadcast it, you consume it.

AI broke that model. When anyone can generate a polished point of view in seconds, broadcasting insight isn't rare anymore. It's noise.

The new model is thought partnership.

Thought partnership means:
- Co-creating with your audience, not talking at them
- Bringing specific, grounded experience, not generic frameworks
- Sharing what you tried, what failed, and what you'd do differently
- Inviting challenge and iteration instead of presenting finished answers

What makes thought partnership different from thought leadership:

Leadership says: "Here's my framework."
Partnership says: "Here's what I learned. What's your experience?"

Leadership says: "Follow these 5 steps."
Partnership says: "We tried these 5 steps. Step 3 broke. Here's why."

Leadership is performative. Partnership is productive.

AI can generate thought leadership. It cannot generate thought partnership. Because partnership requires lived experience, vulnerability, specificity, and a willingness to be wrong in public.

That's the new bar. Not "do you have a point of view?" Everyone does now. But "did you earn that point of view through real work?"

Real expertise is grounded, specific, and accountable. Everything else is noise with better formatting.

Be a thought partner.
