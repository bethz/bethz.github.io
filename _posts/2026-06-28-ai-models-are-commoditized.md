Everyone has access to the same foundation models. GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama. The model isn't your advantage. It never was.

Your advantage is what you ground it in.

Without your data, your domain expertise, your customer context, AI is just a very articulate guesser. It produces generic answers to specific problems. It sounds smart while saying nothing unique.

I've watched teams spend months evaluating which model to use when the real question was: do we even have the data to make any model useful?

The moat isn't the model. The moat is:
- Your proprietary data pipelines
- Your domain-specific knowledge graphs
- Your people who know what "right" looks like in your context
- Your operational history that no foundation model was trained on

Models are commoditized. They'll keep getting cheaper and more capable. That's table stakes.

The differentiator is the layer between the model and the decision: your knowledge, your data, your judgment about what matters.

If your AI strategy is "pick the best model," you're competing on something that depreciates monthly. If your AI strategy is "build the best grounding layer," you're building a moat that compounds.

Invest in your data. The model will take care of itself.
