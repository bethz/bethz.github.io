Anyone can build an AI model. Seriously. The tools are there. The tutorials are everywhere. A weekend and some curiosity gets you a working prototype.

But maintaining and updating it reliably? That's where most teams fail.

What I see in enterprise AI partnerships:

- Teams celebrate the demo, then struggle with the lifecycle
- Models drift. Data changes. The business evolves. The model doesn't.
- "It worked in the lab" becomes "it broke in production" within months

The real AI skill isn't building. It's operating.

Operating means:
- Monitoring for drift before users notice
- Versioning models like you version code
- Having a rollback plan that isn't "rebuild from scratch"
- Designing for update cycles, not just launch day

If your AI strategy ends at "deploy the model," you don't have a strategy. You have a science experiment.

The companies winning with AI right now aren't the ones with the fanciest models. They're the ones who treat AI like infrastructure, not a project.
