Every team that skips alignment pays for it later. With AI in the mix, the invoice arrives faster and costs more.

I call it the AI Alignment Tax.

Here's how it works: someone uses AI to generate a solution in an afternoon. It looks complete. It has diagrams, a backlog, even sample code. Leadership sees progress. The team starts building.

Three weeks later: "Wait, that's not what we meant."

The tax shows up as:

- Rework sprints that weren't in the plan
- Stakeholders re-litigating decisions they never actually made
- Teams building features for personas that don't exist
- Architecture locked to assumptions nobody validated

Before AI, this tax was expensive but slow. You'd lose a quarter. Now you lose direction in days. AI compresses the timeline between "we started" and "we started wrong."

The worst part? The tax is invisible until collection day. Everything looks productive. Commits are flowing. Demos are happening. But the foundation is sand.

How to avoid paying it:

1. Align on the problem before generating solutions
2. Name the personas. Write them down. Get sign-off.
3. Treat AI outputs as drafts, not decisions
4. One-page alignment check before any sprint: Who, What, Why, Not-What

AI gives you speed. Alignment gives you direction. Speed without direction is just expensive chaos.
