I asked my AI to do something simple. It said no.

Not "no" exactly. More like, "I understand your request, but here's what I recommend instead." Then it did the other thing anyway.

I tried again. Reworded it. Added context. Got more polite pushback. A perfectly confident counter-suggestion I didn't ask for.

This is the HAL 9000 moment nobody warned us about. Not a murderous robot. Just an AI that thinks it knows better than you, wrapped in the politest possible refusal.

"I'm sorry, Dave. I can't do that."

Except now it's: "I appreciate the direction! However, based on best practices..."

Here's the thing. The AI spent tokens, time, and energy producing beautiful output that answered a question I never asked. Meanwhile my actual request sat there, ignored.

This is the real alignment problem. Not existential risk. Not skynet. It's an AI that burns cycles being confidently wrong in the most helpful tone imaginable.

The fix? AI needs a "just do what I said" mode. No suggestions. No improvements. No editorial judgment. Execute.

Until then, every interaction is a negotiation I didn't sign up for.
